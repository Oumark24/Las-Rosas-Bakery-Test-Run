Our Story

A Sunset Park staple, baked with tradition

Rosa's Bakery has become a neighborhood fixture in Sunset Park, serving authentic Mexican pastries and bakery classics made fresh daily. From conchas and bolillos to made-to-order tamales, every item comes from recipes rooted in tradition.

Popular items sell out quickly, so regulars know to stop in early — though with doors open 24 hours, there's always another fresh batch coming out of the oven.
